What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising script and no contact form. No cookie is set, which is why you have not been asked about any.

The one thing stored on your device

When you confirm you are over {{min_age}}, that answer is kept in your browser's local storage so you are not asked on every page. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser data removes it, and the Reset age check link in the footer removes it immediately.

{{analytics_note}}

Fonts and images

Every font and image is served from this site. No request is made to Google Fonts, a CDN or any other third party, so no outside company learns that you visited.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address in order to send you the page, and may keep it in its server logs. That is outside our control and is the only place your visit is recorded at all. GitHub's own privacy statement covers what it does with those logs.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you contact us directly we will of course have whatever you send us, and we will delete it on request.
