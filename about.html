Who we are

Two ways to hold a drink.

The name is an argument we never settled.

Irony is the one who stands slightly outside the room and notices. The satyr is the one already in it, playing, and not much interested in being noticed. A good bottle shop needs both: somebody who reads the label sceptically, and somebody who just wants to open the thing.

What that means on the shelf

We buy in small quantities and we sell out of things. That is deliberate. A shelf that always has everything is a shelf nobody is choosing.

If you want a recommendation, ask. Describe the last drink you liked and we will get closer than an algorithm would.

Opening a bottle here

We are a shop, not a bar. We will happily talk you through what is open, what it goes with, and what to do with the two thirds left in the bottle on Thursday.
